About Us

Jenny’s Cleaning is a trusted, family‑owned business committed to providing top‑notch cleaning services to homes and offices across the Bay Area. We pride ourselves on reliability, attention to detail, and the satisfaction of every client.

We use eco‑friendly products and offer flexible scheduling to fit your busy life. From one‑time deep cleans to recurring visits, we’ll make your home sparkle while you relax and enjoy the peace of mind you deserve.
